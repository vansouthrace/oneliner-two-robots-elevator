# Two Robots in an Elevator

A [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-motasimfuad-two-robots-in-an-elevator-8c6aeb8f) bounty entry: one funny line of awkward small talk a robot says to another while they ride an elevator together.

## The entry

> So... do you also pretend to recalculate your route every time the doors close, or is my firmware just socially anxious?

Two machines, one tiny metal box, and neither one willing to admit its social firmware is just a polite loop that panics at 0.4 metres.

## How it works

`main.ts` validates the line is 1-200 characters, then writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
